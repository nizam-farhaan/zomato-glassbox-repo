# Zomato Glass-Box Restaurant Policies

These policies apply to every order placed through our app. Please read them before placing an order. If you have a question that is not covered here, contact support from the Help section of the app.

## Refund Policy

We want every meal to arrive the way the kitchen intended. A refund is eligible when an order arrives cold, damaged, or significantly delayed. A delay counts as significant when the order arrives 45 minutes or more after the estimated delivery time shown in the app. Food that is missing items, spilled, or packed incorrectly is also eligible for a refund.

To request a refund, open the order in the app, tap Help with this order, and describe the problem. Please attach a photo when the food is damaged or spilled, because it helps us act quickly. Refund requests must be raised within 24 hours of delivery. Requests raised after 24 hours cannot be processed, so please report problems as soon as you notice them.

Approved refunds are processed in 3 to 5 business days. The amount is returned to the original payment method. For cash on delivery orders, the refund is credited to the wallet in the app. Refunds do not include delivery fees or platform fees unless the entire order was cancelled by the restaurant or the delivery was never completed.

## Delivery Policy

Our delivery partners work to bring your order to you within the estimated time. The estimate is calculated from the restaurant's preparation time, the distance to your address, and current traffic and weather. You can track your order live in the app from the moment the restaurant accepts it.

Deliveries are made to the address you enter at checkout. Please make sure the address, landmark, and phone number are correct. If the delivery partner cannot reach you after two calls and five minutes of waiting, the order may be returned to the restaurant and the charges will not be refunded.

We deliver only within the service area shown in the app. Delivery fees depend on distance and on the time of day, and they are shown before you confirm your order. During heavy rain, public holidays, and peak hours, delivery times can be longer than usual. We will show a notice in the app when this happens.

## Cancellation Policy

You can cancel an order for free until the restaurant accepts it. Once the restaurant has started preparing your food, cancellation is not possible, because the ingredients have already been used. If you cancel after preparation has started, the full order value may be charged.

The restaurant may cancel an order when an item is out of stock or when the kitchen is unable to prepare the order. In that case you will receive a full refund, including any delivery fees, and you will be notified in the app right away. Orders can also be cancelled by our support team if the delivery address is outside the service area or if we detect a payment problem.

Repeated cancellations by the same customer may affect future access to cash on delivery. This helps protect our restaurant partners and delivery staff from wasted effort.

## Packaging & Hygiene

Every restaurant partner must follow our hygiene standards. Kitchens are inspected regularly, and food handlers must wear gloves and hair coverings while preparing orders. Staff must wash their hands before handling food and after handling money.

Hot food is packed in insulated containers so it stays warm during delivery. Gravies and liquids are sealed to prevent leaks. Cutlery, napkins, and condiments are included only when you request them in the order notes, which also reduces waste. Packaging is recyclable wherever local facilities allow it.

If you notice a hygiene concern with an order, such as a foreign object, an unusual smell, or food that looks spoiled, do not eat it. Report it through the Help section of the app, along with a photo if possible. We review every hygiene report and take action against restaurants that repeatedly fall short of our standards.
